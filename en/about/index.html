Support without added pressure

Pregnancy, breastfeeding and feeding children often come with a lot of questions. You may get conflicting advice, have little time to decide, or feel like you should be doing things a certain way.

My work is to help you make sense of all that information, assess your actual situation, and find an approach that looks after your health without losing sight of your family's reality.

The goal isn't a "perfect" breastfeeding journey, a perfect menu, or a child who eats absolutely everything. It's about making informed decisions, moving forward with confidence, and finding your peace of mind again.

Dandelion Salud Clinic

Alongside my breastfeeding and nutrition consultations, I am also the director of Dandelion Salud Clinic, a centre in Alicante specialising in pregnancy, the postnatal period and family health.

Working alongside physiotherapy, physical training and other health professionals allows me to offer a more complete perspective, and to refer you for further assessment whenever your situation calls for it.

Each professional works within their own field, but we share the same approach to care: listening, individualising, and supporting without judgement.

Training

  • Degree in Human Nutrition and Dietetics
  • Advanced university training in infant nutrition and feeding
  • Postgraduate specialisation in nutrition during pregnancy, breastfeeding and early childhood

What you can expect

My approach to support

Listen first

Every consultation starts by understanding what's worrying you and what you've already tried.

Explain the why

You won't just leave with a list of instructions — you'll understand why each recommendation can help.

Adapt to your reality

Recommendations should fit your schedule, preferences, resources and family decisions.

Support without judgement

Breastfeeding, combination or formula feeding, BLW or purées — the goal is finding what's safe and right for your family.

Refer when needed

If your situation goes beyond my support, I'll point you towards the right professional.
