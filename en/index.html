Frequently asked questions

Questions before booking

How do I know which consultation I need?

Briefly tell me what is worrying you and what stage you are at. I will help you work out whether you need breastfeeding support, urgent breastfeeding support, complementary feeding, child nutrition or pregnancy nutrition.

When should I ask for breastfeeding support?

You can ask for help to prepare during pregnancy, or when pain, cracked nipples, latching difficulties, concerns about milk supply, expressing, combination feeding, returning to work or weaning come up.

How does urgent breastfeeding support by video call work?

If something is not going well and you need help now, message me on WhatsApp. We will arrange a video call to see what is happening, try some practical adjustments and give you clear guidance.

Can the consultation take place entirely in English?

Yes. The whole consultation can be in English, from the first contact to the explanations, written recommendations and follow-up.

Are consultations available in person and online?

Yes. You can come in person in Alicante or have the consultation online from anywhere in Spain.

When should complementary feeding begin?

It generally starts around six months, when your baby shows the signs of developmental readiness. In the consultation we assess when to begin and how to do it safely.

Can you help a child who only accepts a small number of foods?

Yes. In child nutrition we work on selective eating, food refusal, limited menus and stressful mealtimes with gradual strategies adapted to your family.
