Frequently asked questions

Common questions about breastfeeding and child nutrition

What does a lactation consultant do?

A lactation consultant supports families before birth and helps solve breastfeeding difficulties afterwards: pain, cracked nipples, poor latch, concerns about weight gain, or low milk supply.

How does the emergency video call consultation work?

You book the consultation and we connect quickly by video call, whether you're still in the hospital room in the first hours after birth or already at home. I watch the latch live and give you immediate, practical guidance.

Are consultations online or in person?

Both. The breastfeeding emergency service is always by video call so I can help you as quickly as possible; other consultations can be in person in Alicante or online, depending on availability.

Do you speak English fluently?

Yes. I'm comfortable working with English-speaking families throughout the whole consultation, so language is never a barrier when it matters most.
